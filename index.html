About

I am a Philippine-licensed lawyer with more than 14 years of experience. In commercial and contracts law, I advise businesses, NGOs, international organizations, and individual clients on legal and commercial matters, with a particular focus on cross-border contracts and the full contract life cycle from drafting and negotiation through implementation, monitoring, risk management, and disputes.

I practice litigation in civil, criminal, labor, and administrative matters. My practice also includes mediation and other forms of dispute resolution.

I also advise on data privacy, including compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act and National Privacy Commission requirements and GDPR. My work encompasses privacy policy formulation, privacy impact assessments, records of processing, privacy notices, data-sharing and processing arrangements, and the privacy implications of AI and other technologies.

I have embraced emerging technologies, including AI, to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of my legal work while remaining grounded in the fundamentals of the legal profession.

Practice Areas

Commercial Law & Contracts

I practice commercial law, focusing on contracts. I advise individuals, businesses, NGOs, and international organizations on a broad range of contractual and commercial matters.

I support clients throughout the full contract life cycle, from drafting, negotiation, and review to execution, implementation, amendment, and termination, keeping in mind applicable legal and regulatory considerations such as data protection and jurisdictional, organizational, or sector-specific laws and regulations. I also assist with mediation and arbitration when contractual disputes arise.

My experience includes procurement and vendor contracts, project implementation agreements, construction contracts, donor and funding agreements, service and lease agreements, and other commercial and institutional arrangements. I also develop contract templates and use emerging technologies, including AI-enabled tools, to support contract drafting, review, analysis, and workflow automation.

Litigation

I practice litigation across civil, criminal, labor, and administrative matters, representing individuals, businesses, NGOs, and other clients in various legal proceedings.

My work covers legal research, case assessment, pleadings, evidence, case preparation, trial work, and litigation strategy, as well as mediation, settlement, and other forms of dispute resolution. I have handled cases ranging from collection matters in small claims proceedings to litigation involving substantial amounts.

I use technology tools, including AI, in legal research and case preparation.

Beyond my professional practice, I maintain a commitment to legal advocacy involving environmental protection and the rights and interests of disadvantaged communities.

Data Protection & DPO Services

I provide data protection services under the Data Privacy Act, including outsourced Data Protection Officer (DPO) services, training and support for existing DPOs and privacy teams, individual compliance documents (such as privacy notices, privacy impact assessments, and data sharing agreements), and development of data protection frameworks.

Legal Services for Content Creators & Influencers

I advise content creators and influencers on brand partnership and sponsorship agreements, content licensing and usage rights, exclusivity and management agreements, and data privacy and e-commerce compliance, including obligations under the Data Privacy Act when collecting information from customers, subscribers, or followers.

Experience

As an independent legal consultant working remotely, I provide legal services to individuals, businesses, NGOs, and intergovernmental organizations, both local and international, focusing on commercial law, data privacy and contracts while also addressing other legal matters. I also practice litigation.

I worked on contract law and contract lifecycle management, and began working in data protection, as National Legal Officer in the Contract Law Division of the International Organization for Migration (IOM), the United Nations migration agency. I handled institutional and commercial contracts and coordinated with UN agencies, governments, private donors, intergovernmental organizations, NGOs, and other stakeholders in relation to international programs and operations.

I began my legal practice at Jimeno Cope & David Law Offices, gaining experience in labor, contract, corporate, civil, and criminal law. I later joined Raymond Fortun Law Offices, now Fortun and Santos Law Offices, where I strengthened my practice in criminal and civil litigation, contracts, and corporate law.
