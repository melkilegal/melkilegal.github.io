Strategic Legal Counsel

I am a Philippine lawyer with more than a decade of legal experience, focusing on commercial law and litigation. My practice helps businesses, NGOs, international organizations, and individual clients navigate complex legal and commercial matters with practical, risk-based advice and action. My principal area of practice is cross-border commercial law focused on contracts, covering the full contract life cycle—from drafting, review, and negotiation to implementation, management, risk allocation, dispute resolution, and termination.

With my years of litigation experience, I am well equipped to represent clients in trial court proceedings involving criminal, civil, administrative, and labor cases, as well as preliminary investigations, appeals, arbitration, mediation, negotiations, collective bargaining agreement matters, among many others.

Whether representing individuals, businesses, NGOs, or organizations, I am committed to delivering diligent, strategic, and effective legal solutions tailored to each client's objectives.

Commercial Law
Contracts
International Legal Advisory

In my commercial practice, I advise clients on contracts, negotiations, contract lifecycle management, risk management, and cross-border legal issues involving business operations, institutional partnerships, and international engagements. My work includes structuring agreements, assessing legal risks, supporting contract implementation and compliance, and assisting clients in developing and managing relationships with businesses, government entities, private donors, and other stakeholders. I help clients navigate complex commercial and operational relationships across jurisdictions, facilitating effective partnerships and sustainable collaborations.

I advise businesses, nongovernmental organizations, and international organizations on a wide range of legal instruments, including framework agreements, project implementation agreements, donor and funding agreements, commercial contracts, consultancy agreements, partnership agreements, and other institutional arrangements. I support engagements involving governments, private donors, and international stakeholders, providing legal guidance to establish effective partnerships, ensure compliance, and facilitate successful program implementation. I also advise on emerging legal issues involving artificial intelligence, technology governance, AI policy development, and regulatory compliance, helping organizations navigate evolving legal and operational challenges.

I strive to deliver clear, commercially practical, and strategically focused legal advice that protects my clients' interests while supporting their long-term objectives.
