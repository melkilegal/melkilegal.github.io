Strategic Legal Counsel

I am a Philippine lawyer with more than a decade of legal experience, focusing on commercial law and litigation. My practice helps businesses, NGOs, international organizations, and individual clients navigate complex legal and commercial matters with practical, risk-based advice and action. My principal area of practice is cross-border commercial law focused on contracts, covering the full contract life cycle—from drafting, review, and negotiation to implementation, management, risk allocation, dispute resolution, and termination.

With my years of litigation experience, I am well equipped to represent clients in trial court proceedings involving criminal, civil, administrative, and labor cases, as well as preliminary investigations, appeals, arbitration, mediation, negotiations, collective bargaining agreement matters, among many others.

Whether representing individuals, businesses, NGOs, or organizations, I am committed to delivering diligent, strategic, and effective legal solutions tailored to each client's objectives.

PRACTICE AREAS

Commercial Law & Contracts

I practice commercial law, focusing on contracts. I advise individuals, businesses, NGOs, and international organizations on a broad range of contractual and commercial matters.

My experience covers the full contract life cycle, from drafting, review, and negotiation to execution, implementation, management, amendment, renewal, termination, and dispute resolution. I also assist with mediation and arbitration when contractual disputes arise.

I have experience with procurement and vendor contracts, project implementation agreements, construction contracts, donor and funding agreements, service and lease agreements, and other commercial and institutional arrangements. I also develop contract templates and use emerging technologies, including AI-enabled tools, to support contract drafting, review, analysis, and workflow automation.

Litigation

I practice litigation across civil, criminal, labor, and administrative matters, representing individuals, businesses, NGOs, and other clients in various legal proceedings.

My work covers legal research, case assessment, pleadings, evidence, case preparation, trial work, and litigation strategy, as well as mediation, settlement, and other forms of dispute resolution. I have handled cases ranging from collection matters in small claims proceedings to high-stakes and high-value litigation.

Before the emergence of AI, I was already a seasoned litigator. I have since upgraded my capabilities through emerging technologies, including AI, allowing me to combine established legal expertise with contemporary tools to deliver efficient, well-informed, and effective legal work.

Beyond my professional practice, I maintain a commitment to legal advocacy involving environmental protection and the rights and interests of disadvantaged communities.

Experience

As an independent legal consultant, I provide legal services to individuals, businesses, NGOs, and intergovernmental organizations, both local and international, focusing on commercial law and contracts while also addressing other legal matters. I continue to practice litigation with enthusiasm, finding trial work both engaging and intellectually challenging.

I solidified my expertise in contract law and contract lifecycle management as National Legal Officer in the Contract Law Division of the International Organization for Migration (IOM), the United Nations migration agency. I handled institutional and commercial contracts and coordinated with UN agencies, governments, private donors, intergovernmental organizations, NGOs, and other stakeholders in relation to international programs and operations.

I began my legal practice at Jimeno Cope & David Law Offices, gaining experience in labor, contract, corporate, civil, and criminal law. On my first day, the Jimenos sent me directly to court, armed only with a night’s preparation and my law school training—an experience that set the tone for my legal practice: tenacity and courage. I later joined Raymond Fortun Law Offices, now Fortun and Santos Law Offices, where I strengthened my practice in criminal and civil litigation, contracts, and corporate law.
