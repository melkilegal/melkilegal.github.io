ABOUT

Strategic Legal Counsel

I am a Philippine lawyer with more than a decade of experience providing strategic legal counsel and effective legal representation to individuals, businesses, nongovernmental organizations, and international organizations. My practice combines trial court experience with legal advisory work, enabling me to help clients navigate disputes, manage risk, and develop practical, results-oriented solutions.

My litigation practice focuses on criminal law and civil litigation. I also advise clients on labor and employment, commercial contracts, international legal advisory, data privacy, regulatory compliance, artificial intelligence governance, and emerging technologies.

Whether representing clients before the courts or advising organizations on complex legal matters, I am committed to delivering practical, dependable, and results-oriented legal solutions tailored to each client's objectives.
